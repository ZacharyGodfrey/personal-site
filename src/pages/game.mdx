## {frontmatter.title}

This game is currently in the earliest stages of development; it doesn't even have a working title yet. You can play the game in your browser [here](https://zg-game.netlify.app) to see the current state.

### Direction

- Single-player, offline-only
- 3D, first-person perspective
- Warm lighting and colors with low-poly geometry
- Lofi soundtrack with nature sounds (water, birds, breeze)
- Environment: rocky canyon inspired by Halo's Blood Gulch, Beaver Creek, and High Ground maps
- Level layout: main loop with smaller loops and branches inspired by the layout of several zoos
- Hidden collectibles similar to Halo's skulls
- Single level with progress checkpoints
